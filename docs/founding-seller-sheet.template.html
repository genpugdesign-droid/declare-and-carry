How an order works

  1. A shopper orders on declareandcarry.com and pays up front.
  2. You get an email with the items and the shipping address.
  3. You pack and ship within 2 business days, using your usual carrier, and reply with the tracking number.
  4. We handle the shopper: confirmations, tracking updates and customer questions.
  5. You get paid every Friday for orders shipped the week before, minus 12%. Shipping the shopper paid is passed to you in full.
